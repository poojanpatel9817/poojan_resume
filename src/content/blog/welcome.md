# Welcome

I am excited to launch this new website to share my research and teaching activities. 

My work primarily focuses on how **Generative AI** and **Natural Language Processing** (NLP) are transforming the way we analyze corporate disclosures. As the volume of textual data in finance grows, leveraging computational tools becomes essential for understanding market dynamics and corporate behavior.

### What to expect here
On this site, you will find:
- Updates on my latest **research** and publications.
- Information about the **courses** I teach at Bryant University.
- Occasional **blog posts** where I dive deeper into technical aspects of finance and AI.
